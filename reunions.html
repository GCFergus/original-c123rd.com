4th Marine Division Association Reunions

The site of the 2007 reunion will be Louisville, KY from September 5 - September 9, 2007. Following is some info regarding the reunion hotel.

Executive West Hotel
830 Phillips Lane
Louisville KY, 40209-1387
Telephone:800-626-2708

Our reunion hotel, the Executive West Hotel, is across the street from Six Flags Kentucky Kingdom and the Kentucky Expo Center. The hotel is convenient to downtown attractions and virtually next to every major destination in the Louisville metro area. The hotel is only two minutes away from the airport and offers complimentary 24 hour shuttle service to/from the airport. Room rates are $75.00 plus 15.01 % tax and ground level parking is free. Call 1-800-626-2708 for reservations. Be sure to ask for the 4th Marine Division Association special rate. Driving directions are as follows:

From I-65: Either direction (north/south) take exit 131-B. Follow the signs to the Kentucky Exposition Center. Turn left at the first stop light, at Phillips Lane. The Executive West Hotel is the first hotel on the left.

From I-64: Take the Watterson Expressway (I-264) West. Take exit 11, following signs to the Kentucky Exposition Center Main Gate. Turn left at the first stop light, at Phillips Lane. The Executive West Hotel is on the left.

From I-264 East: Take Exit 12, following signs to the Kentucky Exposition Center Main Gate. Turn left at the first stop light, at Phillips Lane. The Executive West Hotel is on the left.

From I-264 West: Take Exit 11, following signs to the Kentucky Exposition Center Main Gate. Turn left at the first stop light, at Phillips Lane. The Executive West Hotel is on the left.
