C-1-23

Muster Roll Notes for November 1943

Total “C” Company notes:

                F – 40

                G – 183

                H – 1

                I – 2

 

 

Note “F”

9, embarked aboard USS ELMORE; 14, sailed from San Diego, Calif.; 15-21, participated in Regimental Maneuvers; 22, arrived and disembarked in the vicinity of Oceanside, Calif.

 

Note “G”

12, embarked aboard USS ELMORE; 14, sailed from San Diego, Calif.; 15-21, participated in Regimental Maneuvers; 22, arrived and disembarked in the vicinity of Oceanside, Calif.

 

Note “H”

9, embarked aboard USS ELMORE; 14, sailed from San Diego, Calif.; 15-21, participated in Regimental Maneuvers; 22, arrived and disembarked at San Diego, Calif.

 

Note “I”

12, embarked aboard USS ELMORE; 14, sailed from San Diego, Calif.; 15-21, participated in Regimental Maneuvers; 22, arrived and disembarked at San Diego, Calif.

 

 

(An additional 10 men (total) on “C” Company Muster were on leave, temporarily detached, sick, or in the brig.)
