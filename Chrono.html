C-1-23 Chronology

Company C Chronolgy

Activated 22 July 1942 at New River, North Carolina as Company C, 1st Battalion, 23rd Marines
Assigned during February 1943 to the 4th Marine Division
Relocated during July 1943 to Camp Pendleton, California
Deployed during February 1944 to Kwajalein Atoll
Participated in the following World War II campaigns:

Roi-Namur
Saipan
Tinian
Iwo Jima

Redeployed during April 1945 to Maui, Territory of Hawaii

Relocated during October 1945 to Camp Pendleton, California
Deactivated 15 November 1945
Reactivated 1 July 1962 at Houston, Texas
Assigned to the 4th Marine Division, United States Marine Corps Reserve
